# Product Overview

Sachain is a blockchain-based fundraising platform that enables entrepreneurs to tokenize project shares using Hedera Token Service (HTS). The platform creates a decentralized marketplace where investors can buy, sell, and trade tokenized shares securely.

## Core Features

**For Entrepreneurs:**

- Project tokenization and share issuance via Hedera Token Service
- Fundraising campaign management with goals and timelines
- KYC/AML verification and compliance
- Real-time analytics dashboard
- Automated smart contract operations (dividends, voting)

**For Investors:**

- Secure marketplace for tokenized shares
- Portfolio tracking and transaction history
- Secondary trading capabilities
- Governance participation through voting rights
- Automated dividend distribution

**Shared Features:**

- Wallet management with mobile money integration
- Transaction history on Hedera DLT
- Email notifications
- Chatbot and messaging system

## Target Users

- **Entrepreneurs**: Seeking capital through tokenized share offerings
- **Investors**: Looking for early-stage investment opportunities with liquidity
- **Regulators**: Requiring compliance with KYC/AML standards

## Key Value Propositions

- Democratizes access to early-stage investments
- Provides liquidity for traditionally illiquid assets
- Ensures transparency through blockchain technology
- Automates complex financial operations via smart contracts
